## About Me

Hello, I am Kyungbeom Kim. I received my M.S. degree in Convergence of AI from the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), where I was advised by [Prof. Kyung-Joong Kim](https://cilab.gist.ac.kr/hp/people/).

My research interests include Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning and Autonomous Driving. In particular, I am interested in building cooperative decision-making systems among multiple agents.

Please feel free to contact me if you would like to discuss topics related to my research interests, potential collaborations, or career opportunities.
